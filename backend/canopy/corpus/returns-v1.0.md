# Standard returns

## Return window

Standard returns are eligible through the entire 30th UTC calendar day after recorded delivery. Day 31 is outside the standard return window. A paid, delivered order and returnable items with available quantity and paid value are required. Eligibility is checked using recorded facts and the server clock.

## Payment and order state

Pending, failed, or already refunded payments are ineligible. Cancelled or returned orders are ineligible. Undelivered orders require human review. Missing or future delivery dates, conflicting information, and unsupported currency also require human review. Only USD is supported.

## Excluded items

Gift cards and other excluded or nonreturnable items require human review. There is no automatic change-of-mind refund for excluded items. A human-review decision does not automatically create a refund; the customer can create an escalation.

## Damage and exceptions

Damaged goods, wrong items, and other exceptions require human review, including claims outside the standard window. Never claim evidence was verified. The customer can create an escalation; this does not automatically create a refund.
